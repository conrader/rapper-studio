Support

Answers first · then a human

Stuck? Most questions are covered below. If yours isn't, email hello@boppy.me — include your iPhone model and, if it's about a specific track, its title.

How does Rapper make a track?

In Easy mode you describe the track and pick a genre; the AI writes the bars and a style caption, which you can edit, then generates the beat and vocals as one finished song. In Studio you go beat-first: cook an instrumental, write your bars over it (or ask the ghostwriter), then record the take. Both land in your library with cover art and synced lyrics.

My track says "In line at the studio…" — is it stuck?

No. Tracks are rendered on shared GPUs, and when a lot of people are cooking at once your job waits in a queue before it starts. It usually takes under a minute, occasionally a few. The progress bar moves once rendering begins. If nothing happens after five minutes, go back and tap Drop again — you won't be charged a second generation for a failed one.

Do I need an account?

Not to start. You get a number of free tracks on your device without signing in. Sign in with Apple or Google later to keep your library across devices — your existing tracks come with you.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no subscription and no paywall. When you hit the daily free limit you can watch a short, optional rewarded ad to keep going, or come back tomorrow.

Can I use my tracks commercially?

We don't claim ownership of what you make. Keep in mind the copyright status of AI-generated music varies by country and we can't guarantee exclusive rights — see Terms, section 8.

How do I share a track?

Open the track in your library and tap Share. You get a public link anyone can play in a browser — no app needed on their side.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your library and any shares you created. Details of what's removed and what's kept for abuse prevention are in the Privacy Policy, section 9.

How do I report a track?

Every shared track has a Report control on its page. We review reports and hide content that breaks the rules, usually within 24 hours. You can also email us with the link.

Contact

Email hello@boppy.me. We read everything; replies can take a day or two.
